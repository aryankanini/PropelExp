## Acceptance Criteria
### AC-001: Retention visibility
- **Given**: A user is at intake or an active workspace screen
- **When**: Session status is displayed
- **Then**: It identifies session-only storage and the remaining inactivity period without claiming durable retention

## Edge Cases
