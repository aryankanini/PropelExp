## Story Title
- Evaluate AI release quality
## Status
- **State**: WIP
- **Superseded By**: N/A

## Description
- As a compliance owner, I want quantified AI acceptance evidence, so that a release cannot proceed with missed deficiencies or unsupported claims.
- **Estimate**: 5 SP (40 hours)
## Acceptance Criteria
### AC-001: Extraction performance
- **Given**: The approved valid 100-page acceptance set and one processing job
- **When**: Extraction runs repeatedly
- **Then**: At least 95% of runs complete within 5 minutes excluding documented provider outages
### AC-002: Quality thresholds
- **Given**: The approved evaluation set
- **When**: Results are scored
- **Then**: Provider name, F-tag, and complete SOD accuracy are at least 95%, every miss is surfaced as uncertain, and unsupported facility claims equal zero
## Edge Cases
- A documented external outage is excluded from timing only, not from response-integrity checks.
