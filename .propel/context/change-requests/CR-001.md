## Change Description

Clarify the BRD so the AI layer generates a structured Plan of Correction for each individual Statement of Deficiency without fixing unvalidated section names, replace the current deficiency-detection criterion with a measurable recall target, and explicitly retain human review before POC generation and approval.

### Business Justification

The change removes ambiguity from the MVP's POC and extraction-quality expectations while preserving compliance-stakeholder ownership of the final POC structure. `[SOURCE:INPUT]` Basis: The originating request explicitly defines the required wording, retained validation item, measurable recall threshold, and human-review boundary.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- State that the AI layer generates one structured POC for each individual SOD.
- Keep the five-part POC structure as an open compliance-stakeholder validation item.
- State that the POC structure remains definable and updateable after compliance-team confirmation.
- Replace the deficiency-detection success criterion with at least 95% deficiency/SOD recall on the representative evaluation set, with missed, ambiguous, or low-confidence deficiencies surfaced as human-review candidates.
- Retain the existing 95% field-level accuracy target for provider name, F-tag, and complete SOD text.
- Clarify that extraction metrics do not remove mandatory human review before POC generation and approval.

### Out of Scope

- Defining or naming the final POC sections before compliance-stakeholder validation.
- Changing any other BRD content, scope, metric, or open question.
- Creating or modifying specification, design, user-story, test-plan, or code artifacts.
